Steve Jobs and Steve Wozniak founded Apple Inc. in 1976 in Cupertino, California. 
Apple Inc. released the Apple II personal computer in 1977, which became a commercial success. 
In 2001, Apple Inc. introduced the iPod, a portable media player designed by Jony Ive. 
Steve Jobs served as the CEO of Apple Inc. until 2011, when Tim Cook succeeded him as CEO. 
Under Tim Cook, Apple Inc. launched the Apple Watch and acquired Beats Electronics in 2014.
